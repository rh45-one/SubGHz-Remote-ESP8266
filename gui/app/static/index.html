How protection works
Single-use signed requests. A 60-second armed window. An uninterrupted 0.8-second hold to transmit. Sending, switching apps, hiding the page, or leaving the window locks it.
No plaintext PIN is stored or sent to the backend. Use Tailscale HTTPS and a trusted LAN. HMAC is not encryption. Hardware acceptance is still pending.
